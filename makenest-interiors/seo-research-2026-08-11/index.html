1. Executive Summary

The design searches tested here show low estimated volume in Frederick County: 21 of 38 return zero, 15 return 10, one returns 20 and one was not available. Your Google Business Profile provides a useful starting point for local visibility, with a 5.0 rating from 10 reviews and your address, phone, website and hours present. Together, these findings support giving Maps visibility and reputation attention alongside your website; they do not measure where your enquiries come from. The most useful immediate priority is to make the work you already show easy to explore and enquire about: live pages still link to 24 old addresses that return errors, and /contact also returns an error. Mobile speed also needs attention: the homepage shows nothing for about 10 seconds in the phone tests. You already have Residential and Commercial service pages and a substantial portfolio. Repairing those broken routes is a firmer starting point than adding pages to chase a large local search audience that the keyword figures do not show.

2.4 Recommended Fix Priority

Repair the old links that interrupt visits

Use the 24-address list in section 2.2 to update links on the named live pages. Restore a page or use a relevant replacement when an old address has moved. Correct /how-to-order and /schedule, whose redirects point to the missing /about-18 page. Point /contact at /getstarted so that address reaches the enquiry page.

Prioritize the slow mobile homepage
