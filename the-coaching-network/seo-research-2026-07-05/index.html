1. Executive Summary

The Coaching Network is technically well-built and competes in a healthy, national-demand market — every page audited has a title, a meta description, a single keyword-bearing H1, self-referencing canonicals, complete social tags, and structured data, which already puts the site ahead of most of its Orange County peers on fundamentals. The single most important thing: the real demand for what Maria does lives in service-and-authority terms, not local-geo phrases, and two of those terms are unusually easy to win right now — “executive business coach” (880 monthly searches, difficulty 4, and already the exact phrase in your homepage headline) and “adhd business coach” (110 searches, difficulty 6, a match for your differentiated ADHD-for-business niche). You are positioned for both but not yet claiming them. The biggest technical opportunity is not broken pages — it is that your structured data is the generic default and does not yet tell Google or AI assistants that you are a local coaching business, a named expert, and a set of specific services; the one speed fix that matters is a very slow mobile hero image. Front-loading the terms you can win in a few page titles and adding business, person, and service structured data is the highest-leverage work — and all of it is within your control.

2.3 Recommended Fix Priority

Add local + expert structured data

Add LocalBusiness / ProfessionalService and Person (for Maria Medina) markup — most WordPress SEO plugins do this in a few clicks. It is the highest technical leverage on the site: it tells Google and AI assistants you are a real Orange County coaching business run by a named expert. None of your local competitors have it yet.

Put “Executive Business Coach” in the homepage title and meta

It is already the H1 on your homepage and one of the easiest terms to rank for (880 searches, difficulty 4), yet the title reads “Online Business Coach & Mentor.” Lead the homepage title and meta description with the exact phrase people search and you already display.

Add Service / Offer and FAQPage structured data

Your service pages (and the $1,333 ADHD program) carry only the default page markup, and your FAQ page has great content but no FAQPage schema. Adding Service, Offer, and FAQPage types can earn richer, more prominent search results.

Tighten page titles to about 60 characters

Every audited title runs long (65–81 characters) and may get cut off in search results. Trim each to lead with the single term that page should win — e.g., “Networking Coach,” “ADHD Business Coach.”

Fix the slow mobile hero image

Mobile page-speed is dragged down by a Largest Contentful Paint of 17.8 seconds — a large, render-blocking hero image loading slowly on phones. Compress/resize that image and defer render-blocking resources; desktop is already fast.

Update the legacy social image and add missing alt text

Replace the “Fort-Lauderdale” og:image with a current Orange County image, and add alt text to the one decorative image per page — minor, but it closes the last on-page and image-search gaps.
