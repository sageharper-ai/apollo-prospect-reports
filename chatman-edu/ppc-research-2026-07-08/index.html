1. Executive Summary

This is pre-market ad research — a go/no-go read on whether Google paid search can work for your math-and-science tutoring before you spend a dollar, and the honest answer is no-go. The clearest way to see why is to run the numbers all the way to customers. Measured at the county level — Jefferson + Berkeley County, where you actually serve, not nationally — your best term, "math tutor near me," draws about 10 searches a month in each of your towns (roughly 40 across all of West Virginia), against 18,100 nationally; your AP-subject terms and every town-name phrase come back at about 10, zero, or no data at all. Carry that through the funnel: at roughly 10 to 50 local searches a month across all your terms, paid ads would earn on the order of 1 to 6 clicks a month, and at a healthy 3 to 5 percent website conversion that is about one prospective family every four months at the optimistic end — closer to one a year at the realistic end. A $500/month budget cannot change that, because the demand simply is not being searched; at a roughly $17 cost-per-click it would not even spend itself down. The live local search confirms it from a second angle: not one advertiser is running Google ads on these terms in your market — the national marketplaces (Wyzant, Care.com, Tutors.com) and Blue Ridge CTC compete in the organic results and the map pack, not in paid, because there is not enough local search to justify a bid. The real opportunity the data surfaces is not a keyword to bid on: it is a Google Business Profile in the local map pack — free, and already where local families click — plus local organic visibility with reviews, and converting your existing free Summer Academy interest into paying families. Put your effort there, not into clicks nobody is searching for. The value of this research is the spend it just saved you.

4. Audience + Targeting

Who your local buyer is, and where they actually find a tutor — which, in this market, is the map pack and organic, not paid ads.

4.1 Demographic & Intent Signals

Your buyer is a parent (roughly 35 to 55) of a middle- or high-school student (grades 6 to 12) in the Charles Town / Ranson / Martinsburg area, searching on behalf of their child, often in the evening and frequently on a phone. That mobile, evening, on-behalf-of-a-child pattern is exactly why a Google Business Profile with a tap-to-call number and reviews matters more here than any ad: a meaningful share of high-intent tutoring inquiries are calls, and the map pack is built for exactly that tap.

4.2 Geo Overlay

Your real footprint is Charles Town and Ranson at the core, then Jefferson County and the Martinsburg / Berkeley County corridor — the same area you already serve in person plus your virtual option. Because there is no local paid volume to fence, the geo work is not a bid radius — it is making sure your Google Business Profile service area, categories, and reviews cover that footprint so you appear in the local pack across both counties.

4.3 Audiences to Test

Concrete starting audiences — specific enough to build in the ad platform this week.

Where they convert 1 — the local map pack

Families searching "math tutor near me" and "AP chemistry tutor" see the three-listing local pack first. A complete, reviewed Google Business Profile puts you in front of them for free — start here.

Where they convert 2 — local organic and reviews

A clear "AP Chemistry and Biology tutoring in the Eastern Panhandle" page plus real Google reviews earns organic visibility a marketplace listing cannot personalize.

Where they convert 3 — your existing funnel

The free Summer Academy already brings local families to you. A simple path from that free interest to paid one-on-one tutoring, plus a referral ask, out-performs paid clicks in a market this thin.

Ad-copy compliance note. No regulated-advertising constraints — tutoring is a standard local service. The only copy discipline: do not imply outcomes you cannot back (for example, guaranteed grades), and answer the free-TutorWV objection honestly with your real differentiators.

5. Next Steps

Concrete moves for this week — ordered, and mostly free or low-cost.

Claim and optimize your Google Business Profile to get into the local map pack

The live search for "math tutor near me" in your area shows a three-listing local map pack above the organic results. That pack — not paid ads — is where local families click. A complete, reviewed, correctly-categorized Google Business Profile (math and science tutoring, Charles Town + Martinsburg service area, tap-to-call number, photos) is your single highest-leverage local move, and it is free. Do this first.

Build a real local page and earn Google reviews

The organic field is national marketplaces and Blue Ridge CTC. A clear one-page "AP Chemistry and Biology tutoring in the Eastern Panhandle" page — what you teach, why you over the free state program, one obvious action — plus a steady habit of asking happy families for a Google review is how a named local specialist out-competes a directory listing. Reviews are what the marketplaces win on today; they are winnable for you.

Convert the interest you already have — Summer Academy and referral

You already have a free Summer Academy funnel bringing local families in. Build a simple path from that free interest to paid one-on-one AP tutoring, and ask satisfied families for referrals. In a market with about 10 searches a month, this owned demand will out-perform a $500 paid-search budget every time. Spend your effort and dollars here.

Want a partner in it? We’d start by claiming and optimizing your Google Business Profile and building one clear local tutoring page — a one-time setup, scoped separately — then grow the reviews and local organic that actually reach families in your two counties.
