1. Executive Summary

This is pre-market ad research: a look at whether paid search can work for your math-and-science tutoring before you spend a dollar. The single most important finding is that the hyper-local market is thin on paid demand — every geo-phrased term we tested ("math tutor Charles Town WV", "algebra tutor Jefferson County WV", "science tutor Martinsburg WV") returns zero monthly searches and no advertisers bidding. That is real data, not a gap: families near you do not type the town name; they search "math tutor near me" and let Google supply local results. So the winnable strategy is to bid on broad, high-intent terms — "math tutor near me" (18,100 searches/mo) plus your true high-school niche, "AP Chemistry tutor" and "AP Biology tutor" — and geo-fence them tightly to Charles Town, Ranson, and Jefferson + Berkeley County so you only pay for local clicks. Be clear-eyed about scale: at roughly $17 per click, a $500/month budget buys about 29 clicks a month, so every click has to count. And before you spend anything, your site needs three things it does not have today — a dedicated paid landing page (right now the only path is a free-Summer-Academy signup form), a tracked phone number with click-to-call, and conversion tracking — because ads sent to a homepage with no measurement and no way to call will waste that tight budget.

3.2 Value-Prop Patterns to Borrow or Counter

Recurring angles across the set — the messages your ads should either match or deliberately differentiate against.

  • Marketplaces lead with choice and reviews ("find a vetted tutor near you") — you win instead on being one named, trusted local expert, not a list of strangers.
  • Price is a recurring hook (Care.com's $18–$21/hr anchoring, TutorWV's free) — do not compete on rate; sell the outcome (grades up, AP exam readiness).
  • "Near me" + subject is the dominant local intent — mirror it exactly in your ad headlines and landing-page copy for Quality Score.
  • Nobody in this set is a WV Eastern-Panhandle math-and-science AP specialist — that specificity is your open lane, especially against the free state program.
  • The free TutorWV program is the objection to pre-empt in ad copy — name the difference (specialist, one-on-one, exam-focused) before the click.
What is flagged as owed here. The value-prop angles above are read from competitors' public websites and the live local search results — not from their live paid ad copy. Free tooling does not reliably expose Google's Sponsored slots or the Ads Transparency Center, so we did NOT observe (and did not invent) the exact headlines competitors are running right now. A live paid-ad-copy pull — the actual headlines, descriptions, and landing pages Prestige and the marketplaces are bidding with — is owed as a follow-on and would sharpen the counter-messaging.

4. Audience + Targeting

Who to put the budget in front of, and how to fence it so a small spend stays local and high-intent.

4.1 Demographic & Intent Signals

Your buyer is a parent (roughly age 35–55) of a middle- or high-school student (grades 6–12) in the Charles Town / Ranson / Martinsburg area — searching on behalf of their child, often in the evening, frequently on a phone. That mobile, evening, on-behalf-of-a-child pattern is exactly why a tracked phone number with click-to-call matters: a meaningful share of high-intent tutoring inquiries are calls, not form fills. Layer in-market and parental audience signals where the platform allows, but the geo-fence and the keyword intent do most of the targeting work in a market this size.

4.2 Geo Overlay

Fence tightly: Charles Town and Ranson at the core, then a radius covering Jefferson County and the Martinsburg / Berkeley County corridor — the same footprint you already serve in person, plus your virtual option. Do NOT run statewide or open-radius; in a thin market that is how a $500 budget evaporates on clicks from outside your reach. Because the winnable terms are broad "near me" phrases, the geo-fence is what converts national volume into local, payable clicks.

4.3 Audiences to Test

Concrete starting audiences — specific enough to build in the ad platform this week.

Test A — AP high-school parents

Keywords "AP Chemistry tutor" / "AP Biology tutor", geo-fenced to Jefferson + Berkeley County, Exact match. Your lowest-competition, best-fit segment — start here.

Test B — "Math tutor near me" (fenced)

Test C — Evening mobile + call-focused

Same keywords, mobile-bid-adjusted, scheduled for after-school/evening hours, with call extensions live — designed to capture the phone-first, on-behalf-of-a-child inquiries a form alone will miss.

Ad-copy compliance note. No regulated-advertising constraints here — tutoring is a standard local service. The only copy discipline: do not imply outcomes you cannot back (e.g., guaranteed grades), and answer the free-TutorWV objection honestly with your real differentiators.

5. Next Steps

Concrete moves for this week — ordered, and mostly free or low-cost.

Build a dedicated paid landing page BEFORE you spend

Today the only path on your site is the free Summer Academy signup form. Sending "AP Chemistry tutor" clicks there wastes budget and hurts Quality Score. Build one subject-matched page — clear headline ("One-on-one AP Chemistry & Biology tutoring in the Eastern Panhandle"), what you teach, why you over the free program, and a single obvious action. This is the prerequisite, not an optional extra.

Add a tracked phone number with click-to-call

Your site has email only. Local tutoring inquiries skew to phone calls, especially from parents on mobile in the evening. Add a visible, tracked number and turn on call extensions so a tap dials you — you are otherwise paying for clicks you cannot convert.

Confirm conversion tracking is live, then start small on AP terms

A campaign without conversion tracking cannot be optimized — verify a Google Ads conversion tag and GA4 are firing on the form and the call. Then launch narrow: AP-subject Exact terms + one geo-fenced "near me" ad group, ~$500/mo, and read the search-terms report weekly to prune waste.

Phone number + click-to-call

Conversion tracking + landing-page speed
