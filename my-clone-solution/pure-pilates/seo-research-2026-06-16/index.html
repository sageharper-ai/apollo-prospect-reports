1. Executive Summary

Pure Pilates competes in a genuinely high-demand market and holds its own on content — it matches or beats three of its four local competitors, with only The Refinery clearly ahead — but it is leaving its biggest opportunity unclaimed, and its website is technically under-built across every page. The one thing that matters most: real demand for what you actually do — private, one-on-one Pilates — is substantial and unusually easy to compete for ("private pilates" and its "near me" family pull thousands of low-difficulty monthly searches), yet your site speaks in internal labels ("Privates / Partners") instead of the words searchers type. Underneath that, the technical foundation is thin sitewide: every page is missing a meta description, none carry structured data for Google or AI assistants to read, 88 of 98 images have no alt text, and your linked accessibility-statement page returns a 404. Your one local term with real volume, "pilates summit nj," is exactly where to hold and climb. The encouraging part: nearly every fix here is a content-and-clarity change you control, not an expensive backlink race.

2.3 Recommended Fix Priority

Add meta descriptions — every page is missing one

All 13 pages lack a meta description, so Google writes its own snippet for each. Start with the homepage and the money pages (Services, The Studio, Get Started), leading with "private Pilates studio in Summit, NJ." Free, fast, fully in your control.

Add LocalBusiness structured data

No page on the site has any JSON-LD. Add LocalBusiness schema (name, Summit address, hours, phone) — highest technical leverage, and a first-mover edge since no local competitor has any either.

Rewrite the generic page titles

Eight pages use thin "[Page] - Pure Pilates NJ" titles with no keywords or location. Rewrite the homepage and money pages first, e.g. "Private Pilates Studio in Summit, NJ | Pure Pilates."

Add alt text to images — 88 of 98 have none

Nine of every ten images lack alt text, which hurts both accessibility (your Lighthouse Accessibility score is 83) and image search. Prioritize the homepage and Services images.

Fix the broken + malformed links

Your footer links to /accessibility-statement/, which returns a 404 — restore the page or drop the link. And the homepage link to /the-pilates-method/ carries stray trailing spaces; clean the URL.

Resolve heading structure

The homepage has three H1s and Services has two; /new-booking/ has none. Use exactly one keyword-bearing H1 per page and demote the rest to H2.

Align service labels to searcher language

Reword "Privates / Partners" toward "Private Pilates" — same offering, the words people actually search.
